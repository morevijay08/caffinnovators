# PROJECT CONTEXT — LIFEPath INDIA

## Identity
- Product: LifePath India
- Tagline: Every Interest Has a Future.
- Purpose: help students explore careers using interests, curiosity, work preferences, goals and learnable skills—not marks alone.
- Users: Classes 8–12, diploma and college students exploring future directions.
- Context: Indian education and career pathways.

## Problem
Students can be pushed toward a narrow set of careers from marks, social expectations or incomplete information. Some need a realistic pathway for a known goal; others need safe activities to discover interests.

## Principles
1. Interest first; marks are context, not destiny.
2. Discovery before forced selection.
3. Show multiple plausible options and explain the match.
4. Skills can improve; current difficulty is not a permanent label.
5. Give staged roadmaps and transparent cost evidence.
6. Changing direction is normal; preserve progress.
7. Inclusive, supportive tone and minimal data collection.

## Core journeys
A. Knows interests → onboarding → assessment → explained career suggestions → save/compare.
B. Unsure → Interest Discovery Lab activities/reflection → refined options.
C. Has a goal → career details/pathway → readiness quiz → learning plan.
D. Has subject difficulty → timed diagnostic → topic feedback → practice plan → retest.
E. Changes goal → preserve progress → compare old/new paths → new roadmap.

## Hackathon MVP (coherent end-to-end flow)
1. Premium landing and clear calls to action.
2. Lightweight onboarding.
3. Multi-question interest assessment with timer and saved results.
4. Explainable recommendations across several career clusters.
5. Search/filter career explorer with detail profiles.
6. One complete year-by-year sample roadmap with illustrative costs clearly marked.
7. Timed Math Readiness Assessment with topic tags.
8. Supportive topic analysis, improvement plan and retest.
9. Change My Path while preserving achievements.
10. Dashboard and simple compare flow.
11. Simulated rule-based assistant with clear disclaimer.
12. Scholarship cards only as fictional demo content.

## Out of MVP scope
- Real accounts or real student profiles.
- Database or permanent personal-data storage.
- Paid/API-based AI, live scholarship feeds or required network services.
- Comprehensive verified catalogue of all Indian careers.
- Unverified exact fees/salaries/exam criteria/college claims/official links.
- Separate FastAPI backend or JavaScript frontend.

## Demo content policy
Representative content is allowed only when explicitly marked illustrative. Every career record should support source URLs and a last-verified date for future verified facts. Do not fabricate official links.

## Product promise
Supports exploration and planning; not a licensed counsellor, psychometric diagnosis, admissions authority, or guarantee of job/admission.
